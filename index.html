Every invoice passes three stages before being flagged as missing.
Tier 1
⚡

Exact Match

Matches GSTIN + cleaned invoice number. Strips specials, leading zeros, and spacing — catches INV/2025/001 vs INV2025001 silently.

Features
Everything Built-In
Upload CSV or Excel — no portal access, no manual mapping needed.
🤖

Auto Column Mapping

Fuzzy-matches raw headers from any ERP — Tally, SAP, or custom Excel exports — to the engine's canonical schema.

📨

Vendor Nudge Bot

Auto-drafts Section 16(2)(aa) compliance emails and WhatsApp notices per defaulting vendor with exact invoice lists.

📈

ML Risk Scoring

Isolation Forest anomaly detection plus an explainable 0-100 risk score. Flags High / Medium / Low for prioritised follow-up.

📊

Excel + PDF Export

One-click styled multi-sheet Excel workbook and a boardroom-ready PDF audit report with vendor risk tables.

🗓️

Mixed Date Handling

Parses YYYY-MM-DD, DD-MM-YYYY, DD/MM/YYYY without month-swapping bugs — a silent failure in most naive scripts.

🔐

GSTIN Validation

Warns on non-standard 15-character GSTINs before reconciliation. Surfaces data quality issues early, not at filing time.
